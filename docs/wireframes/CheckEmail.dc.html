Get your first pay app out

0 of 5 steps done. Steps marked optional don't count. You can bring this back from Help.

  1. Add your company address, phone and licence number (they print on every pay app).Company details
  2. Create your first job: contract amount, customer and where pay apps go.New job
  3. Put the contract and paperwork on its record: drop files on the job, or bring emails and texts in through the Inbox.
  4. Optional: Set up a schedule of values to bill line by line (paste it from your estimate).
  5. Record progress, or mark the job done. Duracash drafts the pay app and lists the paperwork the customer needs.
  6. Add the missing paperwork, then Approve & send.
  7. Optional: Already been paid on some jobs? Import the payments from QuickBooks.
  8. Optional: Connect QuickBooks.QuickBooks
