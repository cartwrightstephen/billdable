HOW THE CARDS WORK
  • A card with a suggested job has one button, Add to (job); Other job and Change type open the two selects. Under the select, the item's likely jobs come first.
  • No suggestion: the jobs it could be are one-tap buttons ("Could be A or B (both Northline GC jobs). Pick one."), else Pick a job… and a kind.
  • A text that says a job is done adds "This says the job is done." with Mark (job) done and draft the pay app; Today shows it first.
  • Everything received lists every item that came in, newest first, 50 at a time: when, from whom, how it came in, what became of it and how Duracash matched it. Filter by how it came in, status and job; sort by date, sender or job. Undo takes back a dismiss or an add; Print prints the one item.
ON A PHONE, AND FOR MEMBERS
  • Files dropped or pasted on any page but a job's land in Waiting. The file box, Which job? and the paste box are on Import.
  • The home-screen shortcut Take a photo opens /communications?camera=1 with the Inbox's Take a photo button (on a phone only) in focus. Files shared to Duracash while signed out wait 30 minutes: after sign-in every page offers Finish adding N shared items or Discard.
  • Bring emails and texts in automatically: owners see the steps and the private web addresses with Copy and Make a new address; members see "An owner connects the email service and the texting number. Items sent to them wait here to be reviewed before filing."
