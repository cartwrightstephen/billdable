Lien waivers

Which type?
  • Conditional, progress payment. Before the money arrives: it counts only once their payment clears.
  • Unconditional, progress payment. You already have the money for this pay app.
  • Conditional, final payment. For the last payment, before it arrives: it counts once the payment clears.
  • Unconditional, final payment. You have the last payment, and nothing more is owed on the job.
