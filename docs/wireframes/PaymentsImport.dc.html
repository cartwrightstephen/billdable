Payments from QuickBooks

In QuickBooks, export a report to CSV and paste it here. Any QuickBooks report with payments in it works; invoices and refunds are skipped. Duracash proposes a pay app for each payment and records nothing until you tick and apply. A check already recorded from the same client is skipped, so importing the same file twice is safe.
