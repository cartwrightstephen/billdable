WHERE THE SUGGESTION CAME FROM

A pay app the client sent back (“Client sent a pay app back? Note why.” on the job page, then Save to the client playbook) or a draft they wanted changed becomes a suggested paperwork item. Nothing changes until Add to paperwork. The demo has no open suggestion; this one is drawn to show it. Each box has an (i) with the trade term, and a wrong value turns its box red with the reason under it.
