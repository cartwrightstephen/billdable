Billing

No subscription yet. The free trial still applies: the first charge comes when it ends.

Charged on the 5th of each month, after your own pay apps are out. Payment by card through Stripe. Duracash never stores the card. Nothing is ever deleted for an unpaid bill; at worst the account becomes read-only.

After subscribing the button reads Manage billing and the status shows Trial or Active with the next renewal. A member sees this page read-only: the trial line, "Subscription: Active." and "An owner of this company manages the subscription."
