This policy explains what information {{ENTITY}} ("Duracash", "we", "us") collects through the Duracash service ({{SITE}}), how we use and share it, and the choices you have. Duracash is a business tool for contractors. When a contractor puts their customers' and payers' details into Duracash, we handle that information on the contractor's behalf and under their instructions.

1. Information we collect

You give us

  • Account details: your name, email address and password. We store the password only as a one-way hash (PBKDF2), never as text.
  • Company and job information: your company name; jobs, contract amounts, schedules of values, pay apps, change orders, payments, waivers and retainage; notes and daily records; and your customers' and payers' contact details (for example an approver's name, an accounts-payable email address and a phone number).
  • Files and messages: photos, PDFs and other documents you upload, and emails and texts you forward or paste into Duracash, including their attachments.
  • Questions to the helper: the question, the screen you were on, your account and company, and whether you marked the answer helpful.

Created as you use Duracash

  • Outbox: for each email approved to send, the recipient, subject, text, attachments and what happened to it.
  • Audit trail: what was done in your company account, by whom and when.
  • Server logs: for each request, the time, the page or action requested (with secret parts of addresses removed), the result and how long it took. Our application logs don't record IP addresses; our hosting provider may record them in its own logs.
  • Cookies and browser storage: one cookie keeps you signed in. Your browser also stores small display preferences, such as a tip you closed. We don't use advertising or analytics cookies, and the app loads no third-party scripts.

2. How we use it

  • to provide Duracash: store your records, prepare pay apps and PDFs, and send the emails you approve;
  • to keep accounts secure: confirm email addresses, reset passwords, limit abuse and investigate problems;
  • to support you and improve the service, including writing better helper answers from the questions people ask;
  • to send service messages, such as confirmation, password-reset and invitation emails, and notice of changes to these policies;
  • to meet legal obligations.

We don't sell personal information, and we don't share it for advertising.

3. Who we share it with

  • Your team: everyone on your company account can see the company's data.
  • Recipients you approve: the emails, documents and attachments you approve go to the addresses on them.
  • Service providers who run parts of Duracash for us under contract: our hosting provider (Render), our email delivery provider, and, if your company uses them, text-message and inbound-email providers. They may only use the information to provide their service to us.
  • Legal and safety: when the law requires it, or to protect the rights, property or safety of our users, the public or us.
  • Business transfer: if Duracash is merged, acquired or sold, information may transfer as part of that deal, under this policy's protections.

4. How long we keep it

  • Company data, files and account details are kept while the account is open.
  • When an owner deletes the company account, we immediately delete its jobs, records, files, Outbox, audit trail, helper questions, users and inbound addresses. Copies inside our backups are deleted as those backups expire, within {{BACKUP_DAYS}} days.
  • Password-reset links expire after one hour, confirmation links after three days and invitations after seven days.
  • Hosting provider logs are kept according to that provider's retention settings.

5. Security

We separate each company's data, store passwords as one-way hashes, keep only a fingerprint of reset, confirmation and invitation links, send everything over HTTPS, back data up regularly, and log requests without secrets. Access is limited to the people who run Duracash. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach that affects your information, we will tell you as the law requires.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Access and export: download your company's data at any time with Export data.
  • Correction: edit your records in the app, or ask us.
  • Deletion: an owner can delete the company account from Team & account, or ask us to.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights, such as to know what we hold about you or to appeal a decision about your request.
